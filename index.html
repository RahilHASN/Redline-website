03 — Industries We Serve

Every sector,
every system.

Commercial Buildings

Sprinkler and hydrant system design for office buildings, retail spaces, and multi-storey commercial developments — from low-rise to high-rise.

Warehousing & Logistics

Sprinkler and hydrant coverage designed for large-footprint storage facilities, accounting for storage height and commodity classification.

Industrial & Process Facilities

Design for manufacturing units, factories, and process plants — including refinery-grade hydrant networks and deluge/foam systems suited to high-hazard industrial classifications.

04 — Process

How a set of drawings gets built.

Four stages, one continuous markup trail from concept to certificate of occupancy.

STAGE 01

Assess

Site walk, occupancy classification, hazard analysis, and code baseline — before a single line is drawn.

STAGE 02

Design

Hydraulic calculations, device layout, and coordination drawings, cross-checked against every applicable code.

STAGE 03

Estimate

BOQ preparation and cost estimation, so budget and design move forward together, not one after the other.

STAGE 04

Deliver

Final design packages issued to NFPA and National Building Code requirements, ready for permit and construction.
